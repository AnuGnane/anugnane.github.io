Shiftie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-23

Shiftie is designed to respect your privacy. Shiftie does not collect, store, or share any personal data on our own servers — because there are no Shiftie servers.

What Shiftie stores

  • Your progress (solved puzzles, best scores, daily streak, hint balance) is stored only on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, Apple may sync this between your devices using your private iCloud account; we never see it.
  • Purchases (theme packs, hint bundles) are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. Shiftie receives only the confirmation needed to unlock content; it never sees your payment details.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple manages your player identity, leaderboard scores, and achievements under Apple’s privacy policy. Shiftie submits your move count to the relevant leaderboard, but does not collect or store your Game Center identity itself.

What Shiftie does NOT do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or email collection.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Shiftie is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
