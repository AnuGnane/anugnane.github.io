Shiftie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Shiftie is designed to respect your privacy. Shiftie does not collect, store, or share any personal data on our own servers — because there are no Shiftie servers.

What Shiftie stores

  • Your progress (solved puzzles, best scores, daily streak) and your settings (sound, appearance) are stored only on your device. Shiftie does not sync them anywhere; like any app data, they are included in your own device backups, which we never see.
  • If your saved progress ever becomes unreadable, Shiftie starts afresh and keeps the old file on your device, renamed, rather than deleting it. It is never sent anywhere.
  • No purchases. Shiftie 1.0 is free with every puzzle pack included; it has no in-app purchases, so it never handles payment details.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple manages your player identity, leaderboard scores, and achievements under Apple’s privacy policy. Shiftie submits your move count to the relevant leaderboard and your progress towards achievements, and shows the leaderboard’s top players as Game Center provides them, but does not collect or store your Game Center identity, or anyone else’s, itself.

What Shiftie does NOT do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No accounts, sign-ups, or email collection.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Shiftie is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
