How to play

Slide any row or column of the letter grid — letters wrap around the edges. Line up each target word so it reads across a row or down a column. Find them all in as few moves as you can.

  • Daily — three puzzles a day (one per size) with Game Center leaderboards. Solving keeps your streak alive.
  • Archive — pick a board size and play as many past-style puzzles as you like, offline.
  • Create — build your own puzzle from any words and share it with a link.
  • Stuck? — give up on a puzzle to reveal a worked solution. Every board is solvable by design.

FAQ

How do I restore purchases?
Themes tab → “Restore purchases” (or reinstall and sign in with the same Apple ID).

Is my progress backed up?
Progress lives on your device and syncs via iCloud if you have it enabled.

A shared puzzle link won’t open.
Make sure Shiftie is installed and you’re on iOS 18 or later.
