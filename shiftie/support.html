Shiftie — Support

How to play

Slide any row or column of the letter grid — letters wrap around the edges. Line up each target word so it reads across a row or down a column. Find them all in as few moves as you can.

  • Daily — three puzzles a day (5×5, 6×6 and 8×8), with Game Center leaderboards behind the trophy button. Solving at least one keeps your streak alive.
  • Archive — pick a board size and play a set of puzzles that runs from easiest to hardest, as often as you like, offline.
  • Create — build your own puzzle from your own words (3 letters up to the board’s width, each word once, and none inside another) and share it with a link.
  • Stuck? — give up on a puzzle to reveal a worked solution. Every board is solvable by design.

FAQ

Do I need to buy anything?
No. Shiftie is free, with every puzzle pack included, and there are no in-app purchases.

When do the new daily puzzles arrive?
Every day at midnight UTC, the same moment for every player in the world. That is 1 a.m. in London in summer (midnight in winter), 8 p.m. in New York in summer (7 p.m. in winter) and 9 a.m. in Tokyo. Once you have solved all three, the Daily tab counts down to the next set. The date at the top of the Daily tab is your local date, so for part of the day, depending on your time zone, it can differ from the day the puzzles belong to.

How does my streak count?
Your streak is the number of days in a row on which you solved at least one daily puzzle. A puzzle counts for the day it belongs to, not the moment you finish it, so a puzzle you start before midnight UTC and finish after it still counts for its own day. Until you solve today’s puzzle, the streak shown is yesterday’s, still alive; miss a whole day and it starts again from zero.

How do I sign in to Game Center?
Tap Connect on the Game Center card in the You tab, or on the leaderboard behind the trophy button on Daily, and sign in with your Apple Account on the Game Center sheet that opens. The You tab then shows “Signed in as” with your name, and your daily scores and achievements are reported from then on. If no sheet opens, or the card says “Sign in from Settings › Game Center”, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Game Center, and sign in there. Then return to Shiftie (close and reopen it if it still says “Not signed in”). Leaderboards and achievements need Game Center; everything else works without it.

How do I turn the sound off?
Open the You tab and switch off Sound effects under Sound. Shiftie’s sounds also follow the ring/silent switch and never stop music or podcasts you are playing.

Which accessibility settings does Shiftie support?

  • Text size (Dynamic Type): menus, word lists and captions follow your text size setting in Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size; the board’s letters scale with the board, and the biggest scores stop growing at the largest sizes so they stay on screen.
  • Dark mode: Shiftie follows your iPhone’s light or dark setting, or choose System, Light or Dark under Appearance in the You tab.
  • Differentiate Without Color: with Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Differentiate Without Color on, the board adds a shape to each tile state that is otherwise shown only by its fill: a dashed border on a word about to land, an underline on a solved word, a double ring on a line one slide from a word. Found words always get a checkmark and a strikethrough, whatever the setting.
  • Increase Contrast: with Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Increase Contrast on, Shiftie darkens its secondary text and borders (lightens them in dark mode) and thickens the ring that marks a line one slide from a word.
  • VoiceOver: each row and column of the board is read out letter by letter, with Slide left / right (rows) or Slide up / down (columns) in the actions rotor; found words and wins are announced.
  • Reduce Motion: with Settings › Accessibility › Motion › Reduce Motion on, Shiftie turns off its bounces, springs and pulsing effects.

Is my progress backed up?
Progress lives on your device only; it is not synced between devices. Your device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) includes it. If Shiftie ever can’t save, it tells you: “Couldn’t save this solve.” on the result card, or “Progress won’t be saved this session.” in the You tab.

A shared puzzle link won’t open.
Make sure Shiftie is installed and you’re on iOS 26.5 or later. If tapping the link does nothing, copy it, open the Create tab, paste it into Play a code, and tap Play. A bare puzzle code works there too.
