Shunt — Support

Shunt is a block puzzle for iPhone by Anu Gnana. For a question or a problem, email aniruth.gnana@gmail.com. Your progress stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. If you sign in to Game Center, your Daily score and the “First Daily” achievement go to Apple’s Game Center; nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone other than Apple.
