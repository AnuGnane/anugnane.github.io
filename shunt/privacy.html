Shunt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Shunt does not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third party code.

Your progress (levels solved, stars, times, Daily results and any letters you open) is stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database, which Apple operates under its own privacy policy and which the developer cannot read.

Daily uses Game Center. If you sign in, Apple’s Game Center service handles your identity, your friends list and the leaderboard under Apple’s privacy policy; Shunt sends it only your Daily move count. You can play without signing in.

Level codes (Letters) are shared by you, through Messages or any app you choose; Shunt has no server and never sees them.
